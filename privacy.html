Privacy

Quantified Health Lab uses Google Analytics 4 to understand website usage and to measure clicks on the “JOIN ON TELEGRAM” buttons. The Google Analytics property used by this site is configured with consent controls.

Before consent, Analytics and advertising storage are set to denied. If you accept, analytics and advertising measurement storage may be enabled. Ad personalization remains disabled on this site.

Your consent choice is stored locally in your browser so the site can remember it. Campaign parameters such as UTM values and Google Ads click identifiers may be stored temporarily in session storage to attribute a visit and route advertising traffic to the appropriate Telegram invite link.

What we measure

Measurement can include page visits, campaign source information, device/browser information provided by Google Analytics, and clicks on Telegram join buttons. We do not ask for names, email addresses, medical records, diagnoses or other health information on this website.

Telegram

If you choose to open Telegram, you leave this website. Telegram's own privacy practices and terms then apply. Advertising campaigns may use separate Telegram invite links so we can compare how many users joined from each campaign or country group.

Advertising

Campaign measurement may be used for Google Ads performance reporting. Quantified Health Lab does not use this website to build personalised health advertising audiences or to target users based on a known or inferred medical condition.

Your choice

You can accept or decline measurement through the privacy banner on the main page and reopen it at any time using the “Cookie settings” control in the footer.

Health information

Quantified Health Lab provides educational summaries of health and longevity research. It does not provide medical diagnosis or individualized medical advice.
